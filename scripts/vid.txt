ffmpeg -f v4l2 -input_format mjpeg -framerate 30 -video_size 1280x720 -i /dev/video0 ~/video.mp4

-f v4l2	Use the Video4Linux2 camera interface
-input_format mjpeg	Tells ffmpeg to expect MJPEG from your USB camera
-framerate 30	Records at 30 frames per second
-video_size 1280x720	720p resolution
-i /dev/video0	Input device (your camera)
~/video.mp4	Output video file in your home directory

to download in linux pc
scp <hostname>@<ip>:/home/<hostname>/video.mp4 ~/Downloads/
